My Working Process

A structured approach ensures that every project I work on is delivered on time, customized to your needs, and built to modern web standards.

Phase 1: Planning

01. Discovery & Strategy

Understanding the core problem and setting a clear roadmap.

  • Deep-dive into client requirements and business goals
  • Analyzing the target audience and competitive landscape
  • Defining the project scope, timeline, and deliverables

02. UI/UX Design

Translating ideas into beautiful, intuitive interfaces.

  • Creating wireframes and user flow diagrams
  • Designing high-fidelity mockups with attention to detail
  • Reviewing and refining the design based on client feedback

Phase 2: Execution

03. Frontend Development

Bringing designs to life with clean, efficient code.

  • Writing semantic HTML and modular CSS for structure and styling
  • Implementing interactive elements using vanilla JS or modern frameworks
  • Ensuring complete responsiveness across all devices and screen sizes

04. Testing & Launch

Quality assurance and bringing the project to the world.

  • Cross-browser testing and performance optimization
  • Accessibility (a11y) checks and fine-tuning
  • Final deployment and handing over a polished web experience
